U1 / ABOUT

A builder from Thrissur.

I'm Ajmal — a software developer who got hooked on computers the way most people get hooked on a good book. Somewhere between debugging my first C program and watching a motor spin because of code I wrote, I realized this was it.

These days I spend my time building agentic AI systems, hacking on IoT & embedded hardware, and co-founding Nexr — where I lead creative direction and product design. On the side, I'm bringing Atom Innovations and Atom Skill Academy to life, both on the way with solid roadmaps and real traction. The best way to learn is to teach, and the second best way is to build the thing you wish existed.

I serve as Membership Development Coordinator at the IEEE Student Branch, College of Engineering Kidangoor, intern as an IoT Assistant for the PM Vikas programme, and serve as Director & Social Media Head at Noon Academy. I love mentoring students through their first projects, and believe that the best portfolios are the ones you can plug in and use. I'm passionate about making tech accessible and building communities where curious people can learn and build together.

Campus Ambassador  ·  IEEE Day 2026 — 6 October

IEEE Day marks the first time, back in 1884, that engineers worldwide gathered to share their technical ideas. As Campus Ambassador, my job is to bring that celebration to my campus — promoting IEEE Day locally, getting members excited enough to run their own events, and using the day as an open door for students who have never heard what IEEE actually does.

📣 Spread the word

Promote IEEE Day across the student branch and the wider campus so the date actually lands on people's radar.

🎉 Spark events

Nudge members and groups to organise their own IEEE Day activities, and help shape the ideas into something runnable.

🤝 Grow the branch

Share what membership actually gets you — and turn the day's momentum into new members.

🌍 Global team

Volunteer alongside ambassadors from other sections and regions, coordinating a campaign that runs worldwide.

Campus Ambassador  ·  IEEEXtreme 20.0 — 31 October 2026

IEEEXtreme is a 24-hour global programming competition where teams of IEEE student members go head-to-head against thousands of others to solve a set of algorithmic problems. As Campus Ambassador for the 20th edition, I'm the on-campus point of contact — rallying teams, helping them get set up and proctored, and making sure nobody misses out because they didn't know how it works.

🧑‍💻 Build the teams

Reach out across the branch and campus to get students registered and grouped into competing teams.

🎓 Prep & train

Help run seminars, pre-workshops, and practice sessions so teams show up ready instead of guessing.

🏟️ Sort the logistics

Support the student branch in hosting the competition — venue, proctor, and setup for a full 24-hour run.

🧭 Be the guide

Answer the questions — rules, eligibility, platform — for anyone who needs a hand getting to the start line.
