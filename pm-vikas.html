PM Vikas

Supporting artisans and craftspeople through digital inclusion and market linkages.

The Pradhan Mantri Vishwakarma Kaushal Samman (PM Vikas) programme focuses on empowering traditional artisans and craftspeople. My volunteering efforts have centered around bringing modern technological tools to these incredibly skilled individuals, helping them scale their reach and streamline their work.

By conducting workshops and hands-on sessions, we help bridge the gap between traditional craftsmanship and the digital economy. This includes training on digital literacy, utilizing online marketplaces, and understanding modern business tools.
