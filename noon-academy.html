About Noon Academy.

At Noon Academy I wear two hats — Director and Social Media Head. On the direction side, I help steer the bigger picture; on the social side, I own how the brand shows up online: the content, the voice, and the community around it.

The through-line is consistency — making sure everything Noon Academy puts out feels intentional, on-brand, and genuinely engaging.
