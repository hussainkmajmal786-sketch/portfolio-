Overview

What Nexr is.

Nexr is a young venture focused on building digital tools that feel considered — clean, purposeful, and genuinely useful. As co-founder and creative lead, I own the brand identity, product vision, and the overall experience: how it looks, how it feels, and how it flows from a first idea to something people can actually use.

The goal is simple — ship products that are thoughtful by default, where design and function are one conversation, not two.

My Role

What I do here.

🧭 Product Vision

Defining what we build and why — turning rough ideas into a clear direction.

🎨 Brand & Identity

Shaping the visual language and voice so everything feels like one coherent product.

🧩 Product Design

Designing the experience end-to-end — from flows to the details users actually touch.

🚀 Ship It

Translating concept into a real, shippable experience — not just mockups.

What I bring
  • End-to-end creative direction — from first sketch to launched experience.
  • A design-led mindset where usability and aesthetics are decided together.
  • Product thinking: what to build, for whom, and why it matters.
  • Founder ownership — treating the product like it's mine, because it is.
